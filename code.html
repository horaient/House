The HORAI Code.

The standard an Icon must meet, and what HORAI owes in return.

The standard.

  • The Icon standardPresence on stage, mindset, voice and bearing, passion and obsession, action, smart hard work, seriousness, big vision, originality.
  • The pathAudition, then Rising, then panel decision, then the fan base an artist builds as proof of presence, then Icon. The Icon title starts at age 18.
  • Lines never crossedViolation of humanity, intolerance, bias, bullying, racism. Excessive ego. Damaging your reputation without handling it wisely. Breaking the Code.
  • ConsequencesA private warning, then public correction or time off stage, then suspension, then removal. The artist can respond. Fan reaction is information, never the punishment.
  • What HORAI owesStage time and support to every artist. Credit on every piece of work, unconditionally. Promotion as an Icon once presence and audience are built.
  • Under 18Guardian consent for participation and for publishing any photo or video. An adult team member at every rehearsal and event. No paid commercial deal without guardian approval. No approval, no stage.
